1. Spatial Distribution Across Australia

Comparing national biodiversity zones with urban micro-habitats.

National Biodiversity Corridors

Eastern coastal capitals support all nine target species.

Native Bird Magpie, Lorikeet, Miner, King-Parrot.

Invasive Bird Indian Myna, Common Blackbird.

Mammal Reptile Possum, Flying-fox, Sunskink.

King-Parrots prefer eastern foothills; Mynas cluster in inner suburbs.

Use sliders to explore monthly sightings across Australia.

2. Urban Sprawl & Interstate Biogeography

Capital distance and climate shape wildlife communities.

Mynas hug capital centres; King-Parrots prefer outer bushland.

Invasive birds peak inside inner urban capital rings.

Interstate Community Dominance

Eastern mainland states support balanced multi-species communities.

Possums and Blackbirds dominate Tasmanian wildlife sightings.

Magpies dominate Western Australia; Possums lead Northern Territory.

3. Daily & Seasonal Partitioning

Wildlife partition daily hours and seasonal cycles.

Birds peak at dawn; mammals forage at night.

Sunskinks bask near midday; nocturnal mammals emerge late.

Sunskinks brumate in winter; birds surge in September.
